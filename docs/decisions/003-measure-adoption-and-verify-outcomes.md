# 003 Measure adoption and verify outcomes from separate evidence

**Status** Accepted.

Adoption uses eligible operators with a persisted first-use event, divided by eligible operators in the window. Opportunity usage divides attempts by eligible observed opportunities. Reliability divides successful executions by attempts. Empty denominators mean unavailable. Coverage uses observed opportunities divided by the expected inventory, capped at one. The automation definitions link back to workflow candidates.

A diagnostic signal records an observed fact. A proposed explanation remains a hypothesis. Mutations capture the signal text, operator decision, owner, intervention and comparable period without updating the observations. Event creation and audit occur in one transaction. Intervention review locks its own record and guards against repeating the same follow-up period.

Time estimates multiply successful comparison executions by baseline assumed manual seconds per item, then subtract reported rework and human review. The modeled opportunity multiplies the baseline count by assumed eligibility. Applying comparison-period eligible-user adoption is a separate adjustment. These estimates describe the fixture's model.

A verified synthetic timing difference uses directly recorded end-to-end human handling from two reviewed studies. It requires complete observation windows, equal measured volumes matching each observed period, and the same measurement method. The result can be negative. Fulfilment of these rules enables comparison of measured handling time within the synthetic fixture. Attribution to the intervention would require a stronger causal design.
